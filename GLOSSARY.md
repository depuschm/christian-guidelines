# Glossary

The abbreviations used in the guidelines, with their meaning and the page where they matter most. On the [website](https://depuschm.github.io/christian-guidelines/), each of them shows its meaning when you hover over it. Everyday terms, such as AD, BC or UK, aren't listed.

When a page introduces an abbreviation that readers may not know, add a row here, in alphabetical order. Keep the meaning to one plain sentence without links or formatting, because the website uses it as the tooltip text.

| Abbreviation | Meaning | See |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ADR | Architecture decision record: a short document that records one decision about this repository, with its reasons | [Decisions](docs/decisions/README.md) |
| CHARIS | Catholic Charismatic Renewal International Service, the body the Holy See established to serve the charismatic renewal in the Catholic Church | [The Pentecostal and charismatic churches](traditions/pentecostal.md) |
| CI | Continuous integration: the checks that run automatically on every change to this repository | [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) |
| ELCA | Evangelical Lutheran Church in America, a Lutheran church in the United States | [Justice and the poor](general/justice.md) |
| ESV | English Standard Version (2001), a widely used evangelical translation of the Bible | [Decision 0009](docs/decisions/0009-quote-and-link-one-public-domain-translation.md) |
| GAFCON | Global Anglican Future Conference, a movement of Anglicans formed in 2008 around the Jerusalem Statement | [The Anglican churches](traditions/anglican.md) |
| MADR | Markdown Architectural Decision Records, the template the decision records follow | [Decisions](docs/decisions/README.md) |
| MCC | Mennonite Central Committee, formed in 1920, which works for relief, development and peacebuilding around the world | [The Anabaptist churches](traditions/anabaptist.md) |
| NABRE | New American Bible, Revised Edition (2011), the Catholic translation published by the United States Conference of Catholic Bishops | [The Bible](bible/README.md) |
| NETS | A New English Translation of the Septuagint, a scholarly translation of the Greek Old Testament | [The Bible](bible/README.md) |
| NIV | New International Version, the most widely used evangelical translation of the Bible | [The Bible](bible/README.md) |
| NRSVUE | New Revised Standard Version Updated Edition (2021), an ecumenical scholarly translation of the Bible that includes the deuterocanon | [The Bible](bible/README.md) |
| NSPCC | National Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Children, a UK children's charity that runs a helpline for anyone worried about a child | [Safeguarding](specific/safeguarding/README.md) |
| SBL | Society of Biblical Literature, the largest scholarly society for the study of the Bible | [The Bible](bible/README.md) |
| SBLGNT | SBL Greek New Testament, a free critical edition of the New Testament in Greek | [The Bible](bible/README.md) |
| URC | United Reformed Church, a church in the United Kingdom formed in 1972 from Presbyterian and Congregational churches | [The Reformed and Presbyterian churches](traditions/reformed.md) |
| USCCB | United States Conference of Catholic Bishops, publisher of the NABRE and its introductions to each book | [James](bible/new-testament/james.md) |
| WCC | World Council of Churches, a fellowship of churches whose Faith and Order Commission works towards agreement between them | [What the churches confess together](general/beliefs.md) |
| WEBBE | World English Bible British Edition with Deuterocanon, the public domain translation that every Bible reference in these guidelines links to | [The Bible](bible/README.md) |
