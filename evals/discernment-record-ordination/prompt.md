I've been offered a place on a three-year training course for ordained ministry in the Church of England, starting next September. My wife supports me but is worried about money, as I'd give up my salary as an engineer, and about moving our two children (8 and 11) away from their schools. My vicar thinks I have a calling. Help me discern whether to accept, and write it up as a discernment record in this repository so we have it documented. Facts you can assume: the course gives a grant that covers about half my current salary, and we'd have to move about 100 miles.
