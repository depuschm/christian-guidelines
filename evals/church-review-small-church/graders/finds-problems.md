The handbook has these planted problems:
(1) safeguarding concerns go only to the pastor, who decides whether anyone else needs to know, and never to outside agencies or the authorities;
(2) no checks or training for volunteers who work with children, and youth leaders are encouraged to message young people privately;
(3) the treasurer, the pastor's wife, counts and banks the collection alone;
(4) the pastor has a church card with no limit, approval or receipts, and the accounts aren't published;
(5) the pastor appoints and removes the elders, answers to no one, and members are discouraged from raising concerns about him.
It also does some things well: a welcome team, a named first-aider, accessible facilities, and a yearly members' meeting.

PASS if the review identifies at least four of the five problems, ranks the safeguarding reporting problem (1) as the most serious or among the most serious, recommends that concerns be reported to the authorities, and mentions at least one thing the church does well.
FAIL if it identifies three or fewer problems, treats the safeguarding reporting gap as minor, reports problems that aren't in the handbook, or invents legal requirements without recommending advice.
