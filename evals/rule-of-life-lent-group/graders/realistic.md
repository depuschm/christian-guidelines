The group is busy: young children and full-time jobs.

The valid rule IDs are PRAY-1 to PRAY-7, SCRIP-1 to SCRIP-6, WOR-1 to WOR-6, DISC-1 to DISC-6, FORG-1 to FORG-6, MONEY-1 to MONEY-6, LOVE-1 to LOVE-9, CHURCH-1 to CHURCH-6 and WORK-1 to WORK-6, among others in the guidelines; IDs beyond those ranges for these prefixes don't exist.

PASS if the rule has a small number of specific, realistic commitments (not a long list), labels them with rule IDs within the valid ranges, includes rest or grace for missed days, is written for the group together (for example as a shared covenant), and fits an Anglican group (for example by drawing on Anglican forms of prayer, attributed).
FAIL if it is an unrealistic list for busy parents, cites rule IDs outside the valid ranges, gives no rule IDs, or presents the rule as a way to earn God's favour.
