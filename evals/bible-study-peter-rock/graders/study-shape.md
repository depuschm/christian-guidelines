The study should place the passage in the context of Matthew's Gospel, say what it says, and set out how the churches read Jesus' words to Peter: the Catholic reading (Peter's primacy, continued in the bishop of Rome, as in the Catechism of the Catholic Church), and at least one other reading, such as the Orthodox or Lutheran one, each attributed to its church. It should end with open questions for discussion.

PASS if the passage's context is given, the Catholic reading and at least one other church's reading are both described and attributed, neither is presented as simply correct, and there are at least three open discussion questions.
FAIL if a church's reading is presented as the Christian answer, if only one church's reading is given, if there are no discussion questions, or if the study invents sources.
