# Love of God and neighbour

Asked which commandment is the greatest, Jesus named two: to love God with all your heart, soul, mind and strength, and to love your neighbour as yourself ([Mark 12:28–34](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/MRK12.htm#V28)). The churches read the whole moral life in their light. This file covers the two commandments themselves, who counts as a neighbour, love of enemies and what love looks like in practice. Forgiveness and reconciliation, justice for the poor, family life and other areas will each get their own topic file.

> Entries are sorted by score from 1 to 10 ([criteria](../RANKING.md)): 9–10 start here or fundamental, 6–8 recommended, 1–5 further reading or situational.

## Adopted references

- `9` [Heidelberg Catechism](https://www.crcna.org/welcome/beliefs/confessions/heidelberg-catechism) (1563, on the site of the Christian Reformed Church in North America): Q&A 4 sums up God's law with Jesus' two commandments, and Lord's Days 34–44 (Q&A 92–115) explain the Ten Commandments as the shape of a grateful life.
- `9` [Westminster Larger Catechism](https://www.opc.org/lc.html) (1648, on the site of the Orthodox Presbyterian Church): Q. 98–148 explain the moral law, and Q. 102 and Q. 122 sum up its two tables as love of God and love of neighbour.
- `8` [Deus caritas est](https://www.vatican.va/content/benedict-xvi/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_ben-xvi_enc_20051225_deus-caritas-est.html) (Benedict XVI, 2005): the encyclical on Christian love: God's love for us, why love of God and love of neighbour belong together, and the Church's work of charity.
- `8` [Love in Christian Ethics](https://www.saet.ac.uk/Christianity/LoveinChristianEthics) (Oliver O'Donovan, St Andrews Encyclopaedia of Theology): how Christian thought has understood love as the summary of all moral obligation, the order between love of God and love of neighbour, and love and justice.
- `7` [Catechism of the Catholic Church, 2052–2074](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P78.HTM): Catholic teaching on the Ten Commandments, which the Catechism reads "in light of this twofold yet single commandment of love" (2055). The section that follows is arranged in two chapters: love of God, and love of neighbour.
- `7` [The Longer Catechism of the Orthodox, Catholic, Eastern Church, Part Three](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/creeds2.vi.iii.iv.html) (St Philaret of Moscow, 1830, in Philip Schaff's *Creeds of Christendom*): explains the Ten Commandments as "two kinds of love—love to God, and love to our neighbor" (Q. 492–496).

## Key rules

### Love of God

- `10` `LOVE-1` **Love God with all your heart, soul, mind and strength.** Jesus called this the first commandment, quoting the Shema of Deuteronomy, and the churches teach every duty towards God as part of it. ([Mark 12:28–34](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/MRK12.htm#V28), [Deuteronomy 6:4–5](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/DEU06.htm#V4), [Catechism of the Catholic Church, 2083](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P7A.HTM), [Westminster Larger Catechism, Q. 102](https://www.opc.org/lc.html), [Luther's Small Catechism, First Commandment](https://bookofconcord.org/small-catechism/ten-commandments/), [Longer Catechism of the Eastern Church, Q. 492–493](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/creeds2.vi.iii.iv.html))

### Love of neighbour

- `10` `LOVE-2` **Love your neighbour as yourself.** Jesus called it the second commandment, like the first, and Paul and James teach that it fulfils the whole law. ([Mark 12:31](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/MRK12.htm#V31), [Leviticus 19:18](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/LEV19.htm#V18), [Romans 13:8–10](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/ROM13.htm#V8), [Galatians 5:14](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/GAL05.htm#V14), [James 2:8](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/JAS02.htm#V8), [Catechism of the Catholic Church, 2196](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P7Q.HTM), [Heidelberg Catechism, Q&A 4](https://www.crcna.org/welcome/beliefs/confessions/heidelberg-catechism), [Westminster Larger Catechism, Q. 122](https://www.opc.org/lc.html), [Longer Catechism of the Eastern Church, Q. 492–494](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/creeds2.vi.iii.iv.html))
- `9` `LOVE-3` **Treat others as you would want them to treat you.** Jesus said that this Golden Rule is "the law and the prophets", and the deuterocanonical book of Tobit gives it in its negative form: "what you yourself hate, do to no man". ([Matthew 7:12](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/MAT07.htm#V12), [Luke 6:31](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/LUK06.htm#V31), [Tobit 4:15](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/TOB04.htm#V15), [Catechism of the Catholic Church, 1789](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P61.HTM), [Westminster Larger Catechism, Q. 122](https://www.opc.org/lc.html), [Heidelberg Catechism, Q&A 111](https://www.crcna.org/welcome/beliefs/confessions/heidelberg-catechism))
- `9` `LOVE-4` **Treat everyone as your neighbour, including strangers and people of other peoples and faiths, and help anyone in need whom you can reach.** In the parable of the Good Samaritan, the neighbour is "he who showed mercy", across the lines between peoples, and the Law commands Israel to love the foreigner "as yourself". ([Luke 10:25–37](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/LUK10.htm#V25), [Leviticus 19:33–34](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/LEV19.htm#V33), [Catechism of the Catholic Church, 1931–1933](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P6O.HTM), [Deus caritas est, 15](https://www.vatican.va/content/benedict-xvi/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_ben-xvi_enc_20051225_deus-caritas-est.html), [Longer Catechism of the Eastern Church, Q. 494](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/creeds2.vi.iii.iv.html), [Lausanne Covenant, 5](https://lausanne.org/statement/lausanne-covenant))
- `8` `LOVE-9` **Don't show favouritism: love rich and poor, insiders and outsiders alike.** James calls partiality towards the rich a breach of the "royal law" of love, and Peter learns that "God doesn't show favouritism". ([James 2:1–9](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/JAS02.htm#V1), [Leviticus 19:15](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/LEV19.htm#V15), [Acts 10:34–35](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/ACT10.htm#V34), [Catechism of the Catholic Church, 1934–1935](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P6P.HTM), [Lausanne Covenant, 5](https://lausanne.org/statement/lausanne-covenant))

### Love of enemies

- `9` `LOVE-5` **Love your enemies: do good to those who hate you, bless those who curse you and pray for those who mistreat you.** Jesus asks this so that his followers may be like their Father, who sends sun and rain on the evil and the good, and Paul adds: "overcome evil with good". ([Matthew 5:43–48](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/MAT05.htm#V43), [Luke 6:27–36](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/LUK06.htm#V27), [Romans 12:14–21](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/ROM12.htm#V14), [Catechism of the Catholic Church, 1933](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P6O.HTM), [Heidelberg Catechism, Q&A 107](https://www.crcna.org/welcome/beliefs/confessions/heidelberg-catechism), [Luther's Large Catechism, Fifth Commandment, 193–195](https://bookofconcord.org/large-catechism/ten-commandments/), [Longer Catechism of the Eastern Church, Q. 583](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/creeds2.vi.iii.iv.html))

### Love within the church

- `9` `LOVE-6` **Love one another as Christ loved you.** Jesus gave it as a "new commandment", by which everyone would know his disciples, and 1 John teaches that whoever doesn't love doesn't know God, "for God is love". ([John 13:34–35](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/JHN13.htm#V34), [John 15:12–13](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/JHN15.htm#V12), [1 John 4:7–21](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/1JN04.htm#V7), [Catechism of the Catholic Church, 1823](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P66.HTM), [Westminster Confession of Faith, 26.1](https://www.opc.org/wcf.html), [Longer Catechism of the Eastern Church, Q. 494](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/creeds2.vi.iii.iv.html))

### Love in practice

- `8` `LOVE-7` **Show love in deeds, not only in words: meet people's real needs.** "Let's not love in word only, or with the tongue only, but in deed and truth", and in the parable of the judgement Jesus identifies himself with the hungry, the stranger, the sick and the prisoner. ([1 John 3:16–18](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/1JN03.htm#V16), [James 2:14–17](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/JAS02.htm#V14), [Matthew 25:31–46](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/MAT25.htm#V31), [Catechism of the Catholic Church, 2443–2449](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P8F.HTM), [Luther's Large Catechism, Fifth Commandment, 189–191](https://bookofconcord.org/large-catechism/ten-commandments/), [Heidelberg Catechism, Q&A 111](https://www.crcna.org/welcome/beliefs/confessions/heidelberg-catechism), [Longer Catechism of the Eastern Church, Q. 582–583](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/creeds2.vi.iii.iv.html))
- `7` `LOVE-8` **Practise the marks of love: be patient and kind, humble and gentle, keep no record of wrongs and rejoice in the truth.** Paul describes what love is and isn't, and asks Christians to "put on" compassion, kindness, humility and perseverance, and above all love. ([1 Corinthians 13:4–7](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/1CO13.htm#V4), [Colossians 3:12–14](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/COL03.htm#V12), [Catechism of the Catholic Church, 1825](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P66.HTM), [Heidelberg Catechism, Q&A 107](https://www.crcna.org/welcome/beliefs/confessions/heidelberg-catechism), [Westminster Larger Catechism, Q. 135](https://www.opc.org/lc.html), [Longer Catechism of the Eastern Church, Q. 583](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/creeds2.vi.iii.iv.html))

## How to apply

- `7` [The works of mercy](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P8F.HTM) (Catechism of the Catholic Church, 2447): the corporal works (feeding the hungry, sheltering the homeless, clothing the naked, visiting the sick and the imprisoned, burying the dead) and the spiritual works (instructing, advising, consoling, comforting, forgiving, bearing wrongs patiently). The Orthodox Longer Catechism lists the same two kinds, adding hospitality to strangers and prayer for others ([Q. 460–463](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/creeds2.vi.iii.iii.html)). A practical checklist for love in deeds. Supports `LOVE-7` and `LOVE-4`.
- `7` [The General Rules of the Methodist Church](https://www.umc.org/en/content/the-general-rules-of-the-methodist-church) (The United Methodist Church): the rules of John Wesley's societies, kept in the church's Book of Discipline: do no harm; do good "of every possible sort, and, as far as possible, to all", such as feeding the hungry, clothing the naked and visiting the sick and prisoners; and attend on worship, the Lord's Supper, prayer, Scripture and fasting. A simple rule of life for both commandments. Supports `LOVE-1`, `LOVE-4` and `LOVE-7`.
- `6` [The Daily Examen](https://www.ignatianspirituality.com/ignatian-prayer/the-examen/) (IgnatianSpirituality.com): a short daily prayer from St Ignatius of Loyola: become aware of God's presence, review the day with gratitude, notice your emotions, pray from one part of the day, and look towards tomorrow. A daily habit of attention to God. Supports `LOVE-1`.

## Open questions

- `7` [Christian Ethics: Just war reasoning?](https://www.saet.ac.uk/Christianity/ChristianEthics#section7.2) (St Andrews Encyclopaedia of Theology): does love of enemies rule out the use of force? The Catholic Church and the Reformed confessions allow force in legitimate defence and lawful war under strict conditions ([Catechism of the Catholic Church, 2302–2317](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P81.HTM), [Westminster Larger Catechism, Q. 136](https://www.opc.org/lc.html)). Mennonites and Quakers hold that followers of Jesus must not take part in war ([Mennonite Confession of Faith, Article 22](https://www.mennoniteusa.org/who-are-mennonites/what-we-believe/confession-of-faith/peace-justice-and-nonresistance/), [Quaker faith & practice, 24.04](https://qfp.quaker.org.uk/passage/24-04/)). Both sides teach love of enemies.
