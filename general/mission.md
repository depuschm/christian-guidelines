# Sharing the faith

Jesus sends his disciples to "make disciples of all nations" and to be his witnesses "to the uttermost parts of the earth" ([Matthew 28:19–20](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/MAT28.htm#V19), [Acts 1:8](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/ACT01.htm#V8)), and every church teaches that mission belongs to the church's very being. This file covers sharing the good news, and the way to do it: with respect for people's freedom and for other faiths, with a life that matches the message, with service that asks nothing in return, and with prayer and support for the church's mission everywhere. It draws above all on *Christian Witness in a Multi-Religious World* (2011), which the World Council of Churches, the Vatican's Pontifical Council for Interreligious Dialogue and the World Evangelical Alliance wrote together ([Christian Witness in a Multi-Religious World](https://www.oikoumene.org/sites/default/files/Document/ChristianWitness_recommendations.pdf)). Treating everyone as a neighbour is `LOVE-4` in [Love of God and neighbour](love.md), and telling the truth is `SPEECH-1` in [Honest and kind speech](speech.md).

> Entries are sorted by score from 1 to 10 ([criteria](../RANKING.md)): 9–10 start here or fundamental, 6–8 recommended, 1–5 further reading or situational.

## Adopted references

- `9` [Christian Witness in a Multi-Religious World: Recommendations for Conduct](https://www.oikoumene.org/sites/default/files/Document/ChristianWitness_recommendations.pdf) (World Council of Churches, Pontifical Council for Interreligious Dialogue and World Evangelical Alliance, 2011): a shared basis for Christian witness, twelve principles and six recommendations, prepared with "the largest Christian families of faith (Catholic, Orthodox, Protestant, Evangelical and Pentecostal)". It is not "a theological statement on mission" but addresses "practical issues associated with Christian witness in a multi-religious world".
- `8` [Ad gentes](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_decree_19651207_ad-gentes_en.html) (Second Vatican Council, 1965): the Catholic decree on the Church's missionary activity: the Church as "missionary by her very nature" (2), charity that extends to all (12), and the duty of all the faithful (36–37).
- `8` [Dignitatis humanae](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_decl_19651207_dignitatis-humanae_en.html) (Second Vatican Council, 1965): the Catholic declaration on religious freedom, including the duty to avoid any "hint of coercion" in spreading the faith (4) and the freedom of the act of faith (10).
- `8` [Evangelii gaudium](https://www.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/apost_exhortations/documents/papa-francesco_esortazione-ap_20131124_evangelii-gaudium.html) (Pope Francis, 2013): the apostolic exhortation on proclaiming the Gospel today, which says that Christians are always "missionary disciples" (120) and that the Church grows not by proselytising but "by attraction" (14).
- `8` [The Mission of the Orthodox Church in Today's World](https://www.holycouncil.org/mission-orthodox-church-todays-world) (Holy and Great Council of the Orthodox Church, 2016): the Council's document on the Church's mission, which must be carried out "not aggressively or by different forms of proselytism, but in love, humility and respect" (introduction), and on its witness of love through service (F).
- `8` [The Lausanne Covenant](https://lausanne.org/statement/lausanne-covenant) (Lausanne Movement, 1974): the evangelical statement on the nature of evangelism (4), the church and evangelism (6), the urgency of the task (9) and the temptations that beset it (12).
- `8` [The Cape Town Commitment](https://lausanne.org/statement/ctcommitment) (Lausanne Movement, 2010): the evangelical confession and call to action, including "Living the love of Christ among people of other faiths" (IIC).

## Key rules

### Sharing the good news

- `9` `MISS-1` **Share the good news of Jesus with others, in word and deed.** Jesus sends his disciples to "make disciples of all nations" and to be his witnesses, and Peter asks believers to "always be ready to give an answer to everyone who asks you a reason concerning the hope that is in you". *Christian Witness in a Multi-Religious World* states that "proclaiming the word of God and witnessing to the world is essential for every Christian" and that witness takes the form of "proclamation of the kingdom, service to neighbour and the total gift of self" (basis 2). The Catechism of the Catholic Church teaches that the Church "strives to preach the Gospel to all men"; the Holy and Great Council of the Orthodox Church that mission belongs "at the core of the Church's identity" and that "the Orthodox faithful are and ought to be Christ's apostles in the world"; and the Baptist Faith and Message that making disciples of all nations is "the duty and privilege of every follower of Christ". ([Matthew 28:19–20](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/MAT28.htm#V19), [Acts 1:8](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/ACT01.htm#V8), [1 Peter 3:15](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/1PE03.htm#V15), [Christian Witness in a Multi-Religious World, preamble and basis 2](https://www.oikoumene.org/sites/default/files/Document/ChristianWitness_recommendations.pdf), [Catechism of the Catholic Church, 849](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P29.HTM), [Holy and Great Council, Encyclical, 6](https://www.holycouncil.org/encyclical-holy-council), [Baptist Faith and Message 2000, XI](https://bfm.sbc.net/bfm2000/#xi), [Lausanne Covenant, 4](https://lausanne.org/statement/lausanne-covenant))

### How to share it

- `9` `MISS-2` **Respect the freedom and dignity of the people you speak with: never coerce, pressure or manipulate them.** Peter asks for an answer given "with humility and fear, having a good conscience", and Paul renounces "walking in craftiness nor handling the word of God deceitfully". *Christian Witness in a Multi-Religious World* warns that Christians who resort "to deception and coercive means" betray the gospel (basis 6), rejects "all forms of violence, even psychological or social, including the abuse of power" (principle 6), and asks that a change of religion come "through a process ensuring full personal freedom" (principle 11). The Second Vatican Council teaches that in spreading the faith "everyone ought at all times to refrain from any manner of action which might seem to carry a hint of coercion", especially "when dealing with poor or uneducated people", and that "no one therefore is to be forced to embrace the Christian faith against his own will"; the Holy and Great Council of the Orthodox Church that mission "must be fulfilled, not aggressively, but freely"; and the Cape Town Commitment rejects "any form of witness that is coercive, unethical, deceptive, or disrespectful", while the Lausanne Covenant confesses that evangelicals have at times "manipulated our hearers through pressure techniques". ([1 Peter 3:15–16](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/1PE03.htm#V15), [2 Corinthians 4:2](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/2CO04.htm#V2), [Christian Witness in a Multi-Religious World, basis 6, principles 6 and 11](https://www.oikoumene.org/sites/default/files/Document/ChristianWitness_recommendations.pdf), [Dignitatis humanae, 4 and 10](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_decl_19651207_dignitatis-humanae_en.html), [Holy and Great Council, Encyclical, 6](https://www.holycouncil.org/encyclical-holy-council), [The Mission of the Orthodox Church in Today's World, introduction](https://www.holycouncil.org/mission-orthodox-church-todays-world), [Cape Town Commitment, IIC.1](https://lausanne.org/statement/ctcommitment), [Lausanne Covenant, 12](https://lausanne.org/statement/lausanne-covenant))
- `8` `MISS-3` **Live in a way that matches what you say.** Jesus asks his followers to let their light shine "that they may see your good works and glorify your Father who is in heaven", and Paul asks that their way of life be "worthy of the Good News of Christ". *Christian Witness in a Multi-Religious World* calls Christians to "integrity, charity, compassion and humility" (principle 3). The Second Vatican Council teaches that the faithful's "first and most important obligation for the spread of the Faith is this: to lead a profoundly Christian life"; the Orthodox Longer Catechism that God's name is hallowed when "others also, seeing our good lives, glorify God"; the Baptist Faith and Message asks for "verbal witness undergirded by a Christian lifestyle"; and the Cape Town Commitment says that "there is no biblical mission without biblical living". ([Matthew 5:16](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/MAT05.htm#V16), [1 Peter 2:12](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/1PE02.htm#V12), [Philippians 1:27](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/PHP01.htm#V27), [Christian Witness in a Multi-Religious World, principle 3](https://www.oikoumene.org/sites/default/files/Document/ChristianWitness_recommendations.pdf), [Ad gentes, 36](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_decree_19651207_ad-gentes_en.html), [Longer Catechism of the Eastern Church, Q. 407](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/creeds2.vi.iii.iii.html), [Baptist Faith and Message 2000, XI](https://bfm.sbc.net/bfm2000/#xi), [Cape Town Commitment, I.6](https://lausanne.org/statement/ctcommitment), [Lausanne Covenant, 6](https://lausanne.org/statement/lausanne-covenant))
- `8` `MISS-4` **Respect people of other faiths and of none, and speak truthfully about what they believe.** At Athens, Paul begins by noticing what the people worship, and the commandment forbids giving "false testimony against your neighbour". *Christian Witness in a Multi-Religious World* asks Christians to "listen in order to learn about and understand others' beliefs and practices", to "acknowledge and appreciate what is true and good in them", and to make sure "not to bear false witness concerning other religions" (principle 10, recommendation 3). The Second Vatican Council teaches that the Catholic Church "rejects nothing that is true and holy in these religions", while it "ever must proclaim Christ"; the Cape Town Commitment commits evangelicals to "refuse to promote lies and caricatures about other faiths"; and the Lausanne Covenant values "that kind of dialogue whose purpose is to listen sensitively in order to understand". ([Acts 17:22–28](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/ACT17.htm#V22), [Exodus 20:16](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/EXO20.htm#V16), [Christian Witness in a Multi-Religious World, principle 10 and recommendation 3](https://www.oikoumene.org/sites/default/files/Document/ChristianWitness_recommendations.pdf), [Nostra aetate, 2](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_decl_19651028_nostra-aetate_en.html), [Catechism of the Catholic Church, 2104](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P7D.HTM), [Cape Town Commitment, IIC.1](https://lausanne.org/statement/ctcommitment), [Lausanne Covenant, 4](https://lausanne.org/statement/lausanne-covenant))
- `7` `MISS-5` **Serve people in need without making your help depend on their faith.** The Samaritan stops to care for a wounded stranger, and Jesus says that the Father "makes his sun to rise on the evil and the good". *Christian Witness in a Multi-Religious World* holds that "the exploitation of situations of poverty and need has no place in Christian outreach" and asks Christians to "refrain from offering all forms of allurements, including financial incentives and rewards, in their acts of service" (principle 4). The Second Vatican Council teaches that "Christian charity truly extends to all, without distinction of race, creed, or social condition: it looks for neither gain nor gratitude"; and the Holy and Great Council of the Orthodox Church that the Church "actively cares for all people in need". ([Luke 10:25–37](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/LUK10.htm#V25), [Matthew 5:44–45](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/MAT05.htm#V44), [Christian Witness in a Multi-Religious World, principle 4](https://www.oikoumene.org/sites/default/files/Document/ChristianWitness_recommendations.pdf), [Ad gentes, 12](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_decree_19651207_ad-gentes_en.html), [The Mission of the Orthodox Church in Today's World, F](https://www.holycouncil.org/mission-orthodox-church-todays-world))

### Supporting mission

- `7` `MISS-6` **Pray for and support the church's mission everywhere.** Jesus tells his disciples to "pray therefore that the Lord of the harvest will send out labourers into his harvest", Paul asks "how will they hear without a preacher?", and he thanks the Philippians for sharing in his work. The Second Vatican Council teaches that "all the faithful are duty-bound to cooperate in the expansion and spreading out of His Body" and that the whole community "prays, works together"; the Holy and Great Council of the Orthodox Church that all Orthodox Churches "ought to participate in this endeavor", praying in the Eucharist "for the whole world"; the Westminster Larger Catechism that in the petition "Thy kingdom come" Christians pray for "the gospel propagated throughout the world"; and the Lausanne Covenant calls churches "to pray earnestly for the salvation of the unreached". ([Matthew 9:37–38](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/MAT09.htm#V37), [Romans 10:14–15](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/ROM10.htm#V14), [Philippians 4:15–16](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/PHP04.htm#V15), [Ad gentes, 36–37](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_decree_19651207_ad-gentes_en.html), [Holy and Great Council, Encyclical, 6](https://www.holycouncil.org/encyclical-holy-council), [Westminster Larger Catechism, Q. 191](https://www.opc.org/lc.html), [Lausanne Covenant, 9](https://lausanne.org/statement/lausanne-covenant))

## How to apply

- `9` [Christian Witness in a Multi-Religious World, recommendations](https://www.oikoumene.org/sites/default/files/Document/ChristianWitness_recommendations.pdf): six recommendations for churches and mission agencies: study the document and draw up their own guidelines for conduct, "where possible ... ecumenically, and in consultation with representatives of other religions"; build relationships of respect and trust; deepen their own faith while learning about other religions; work with other religious communities for justice; call for freedom of religion; and pray for their neighbours. A ready checklist for a church's own code of conduct in mission. Supports `MISS-2`, `MISS-4` and `MISS-5`.
- `8` [Cape Town Commitment, IIC.1](https://lausanne.org/statement/ctcommitment): evangelicals repent of their "failure to seek friendships with people of Muslim, Hindu, Buddhist and other religious backgrounds" and commit themselves to "take initiatives to show love, goodwill and hospitality to them": a practice of friendship and hospitality towards neighbours of other faiths. Supports `MISS-4` and `MISS-2`.
- `6` [Thy Kingdom Come](https://www.thykingdomcome.global/about-us): a "global ecumenical prayer movement" that invites Christians "to pray from Ascension to Pentecost for more people to come to know Jesus", begun in 2016 at the invitation of the Archbishops of Canterbury and York. Supports `MISS-6`.

## Open questions

- `8` [Towards Common Witness](https://www.oikoumene.org/resources/documents/towards-common-witness) (World Council of Churches, Central Committee, 1997): may Christians evangelise members of other churches? The statement traces accusations of "proselytism" between churches to "different understandings of the nature of an individual's church membership and Christian commitment", seen in words such as "nominal" or "re-evangelization", and also accepts that some people move from one church to another "out of true and genuine conviction, without any proselytistic pressure or manipulation". The Holy and Great Council of the Orthodox Church asks for witness "eschewing every act of proselytism, uniatism, or other provocative act of inter-confessional competition" ([Relations of the Orthodox Church with the Rest of the Christian World](https://www.holycouncil.org/rest-of-christian-world)); the Cape Town Commitment rejects "unworthy proselytizing", which it defines as trying to compel others to "join our denomination", while it calls evangelicals to evangelism ([Cape Town Commitment, IIC.1](https://lausanne.org/statement/ctcommitment)).
- `7` [Catechism of the Catholic Church, 846–848](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P29.HTM): can people who are not Christians be saved? The Catechism, following the Second Vatican Council, teaches that those who "through no fault of their own, do not know the Gospel of Christ or his Church, but who nevertheless seek God with a sincere heart" may "achieve eternal salvation", while the Church keeps "the obligation and also the sacred right to evangelize all men" ([Lumen gentium, 16](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_const_19641121_lumen-gentium_en.html)). The Westminster Confession holds that "much less can men, not professing the Christian religion, be saved in any other way whatsoever" ([Westminster Confession of Faith, 10.4](https://www.opc.org/wcf.html)). The Lausanne Covenant says that calling Jesus "the Saviour of the world" is "not to affirm that all people are either automatically or ultimately saved, still less to affirm that all religions offer salvation in Christ" ([Lausanne Covenant, 3](https://lausanne.org/statement/lausanne-covenant)).

## Tutorials and further reading

Teasdale, St Andrews Encyclopaedia of Theology, 2023): evangelism in the Bible and in Christian history, different approaches to it, and criticisms of it, including conquest, "emotional manipulation" and scandal.
