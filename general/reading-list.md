# Reading list

Books, courses and complete guides for going deeper, chosen from across the churches and free online wherever possible. The topic files link to the exact paragraph or question they rely on; this list gives the whole works. For each church's own teaching, start from the [church pages](../traditions/README.md), and for each book of the Bible, from the [Bible section](../bible/README.md).

> Entries are sorted by score from 1 to 10 ([criteria](../RANKING.md)): 9–10 start here or fundamental, 6–8 recommended, 1–5 further reading or situational.

## Complete guides

- `9` [Catechism of the Catholic Church](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/_INDEX.HTM) (Vatican): the Catholic Church's official summary of its faith, sacraments, moral life and prayer, in four parts.
- `9` [The Book of Concord](https://bookofconcord.org/) (1580): the Lutheran confessions, with the three ecumenical creeds, the Augsburg Confession and Luther's Small and Large Catechisms, in a public domain English translation.
- `9` [The Book of Common Prayer](https://www.churchofengland.org/liturgy/book-of-common-prayer) (Church of England, 1662): the Anglican prayer book, with the daily offices, the services of the church year, the catechism and the Thirty-Nine Articles.
- `9` [Westminster Confession of Faith](https://www.opc.org/wcf.html) (1640s): the confession of the Presbyterian churches, with its Larger and Shorter Catechisms on the same site.
- `9` [Heidelberg Catechism](https://www.crcna.org/welcome/beliefs/confessions/heidelberg-catechism) (1563): a Reformed catechism in 129 questions and answers, arranged under misery, deliverance and gratitude.
- `8` [St Andrews Encyclopaedia of Theology](https://www.saet.ac.uk/) (University of St Andrews): a free, peer-reviewed reference work in theology, with entries on the churches, their teaching and their history, which many pages here cite.
- `8` [The Baptist Faith and Message 2000](https://bfm.sbc.net/bfm2000/) (Southern Baptist Convention): the Southern Baptist confession of faith, in eighteen articles.
- `8` [Confession of Faith in a Mennonite Perspective](https://www.mennoniteusa.org/who-are-mennonites/what-we-believe/confession-of-faith/) (Mennonite Church USA, 1995): the Mennonite confession, in twenty-four articles with commentary.
- `7` [The Longer Catechism of the Orthodox, Catholic, Eastern Church](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/creeds2.vi.iii.html) (St Philaret of Moscow, 1830, in Philip Schaff's *Creeds of Christendom*): an Orthodox catechism in questions and answers on the Creed, prayer and the commandments.

## Introductions to the Bible

- `8` [New American Bible, Revised Edition](https://bible.usccb.org/bible) (United States Conference of Catholic Bishops): the Catholic translation, with a scholarly introduction to each book of the Catholic canon.
- `8` [Biblical Hermeneutics](https://www.saet.ac.uk/Christianity/BiblicalHermeneutics) (Richard S. Briggs, St Andrews Encyclopaedia of Theology): how the Bible has been read and interpreted, in the past and today.
- `7` [Bible Odyssey](https://www.bibleodyssey.org/) (Society of Biblical Literature): short articles by scholars on the history and literature of the Bible and its people, places and passages.
- `6` [BibleProject](https://bibleproject.com/): free animated videos and study tools on every book of the Bible and its main themes, made for individuals and groups.

## Courses

- `7` [Introduction to the Old Testament (Hebrew Bible)](https://oyc.yale.edu/religious-studies/rlst-145) (Christine Hayes, Open Yale Courses, 2006): a free course of recorded lectures that studies the Hebrew Bible as an expression of the religious life and thought of ancient Israel, using historical-critical and literary methods.
- `7` [Introduction to the New Testament History and Literature](https://oyc.yale.edu/religious-studies/rlst-152) (Dale B. Martin, Open Yale Courses, 2009): a free course of recorded lectures: a historical study of the origins of Christianity through the New Testament, which, as the course says, doesn't read it as Scripture.

## Classics of Christian writing

All free to read on the Christian Classics Ethereal Library or the Wesley Center Online, in older translations.

- `7` [On the Incarnation of the Word](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/athanasius/incarnation.html) (Athanasius of Alexandria, 4th century): the bishop of Alexandria explains why God became human in Christ.
- `7` [Life of Antony](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/npnf204.xvi.ii.html) (Athanasius of Alexandria, 4th century): the life of the Egyptian monk Antony, who withdrew to the desert to live for God.
- `7` [Confessions](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/augustine/confess.html) (Augustine of Hippo, about 400): Augustine's account of his life and conversion, told as a prayer to God.
- `7` [The Rule of St Benedict](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/benedict/rule.html) (Benedict of Nursia, 6th century): a rule for life in a monastery, on peace, prayer, work, humility and obedience, and on how the community treats its leader and its guests.
- `7` [An Exact Exposition of the Orthodox Faith](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/schaff/npnf209.iii.iv.html) (John of Damascus, 8th century): an account of the Orthodox faith by a monk and theologian of the Eastern Church.
- `7` [Summa Theologica](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/aquinas/summa.html) (Thomas Aquinas, 1265–1274): Aquinas's systematic account of Christian teaching, written "for the instruction of beginners".
- `7` [Revelations of Divine Love](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/julian/revelations.html) (Julian of Norwich, 14th century): an English mystic's reflections on sixteen revelations she received during a grave illness.
- `7` [The Imitation of Christ](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/kempis/imitation.html) (Thomas à Kempis, 15th century): meditations on the life and teaching of Jesus, read for five centuries in more than fifty languages.
- `7` [Institutes of the Christian Religion](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/calvin/institutes.html) (John Calvin, first published 1536): Calvin's main work, important for the Reformation and for Protestant theology ever since, ordered after the Apostles' Creed.
- `7` [Interior Castle](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/teresa/castle2.html) (Teresa of Ávila, 16th century): a Carmelite nun's guide to prayer and the soul's union with God, pictured as a castle of seven mansions.
- `7` [The Pilgrim's Progress](https://www.ccel.org/ccel/bunyan/pilgrim.html) (John Bunyan, 17th century): an allegory of the Christian life as a journey to the Celestial City.
- `7` [The Sermons of John Wesley](https://wesley.nnu.edu/john-wesley/the-sermons-of-john-wesley-1872-edition/) (John Wesley, 1872 edition, Wesley Center Online): Wesley's sermons, whose standard sermons are among the Methodist doctrinal standards.

## Church history and the churches today

- `8` [The Theology of the Council of Nicaea](https://www.saet.ac.uk/Christianity/TheTheologyoftheCouncilofNicaea) (Rebecca Lyman, St Andrews Encyclopaedia of Theology): the Council of Nicaea of 325 and its teaching about Christ.
- `8` [Ecumenism and Church Relations](https://www.saet.ac.uk/Christianity/EcumenismandChurchRelations) (Minna Hietamäki, St Andrews Encyclopaedia of Theology): the ecumenical movement and the agreements between the churches.
- `7` [Church families](https://www.oikoumene.org/church-families) (World Council of Churches): short descriptions of the main traditions and groupings of churches in Christianity and their world bodies.
- `7` [How the Global Religious Landscape Changed From 2010 to 2020](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2025/06/09/how-the-global-religious-landscape-changed-from-2010-to-2020/) (Pew Research Center, 2025): Christians remain the world's largest religious group; how the number and share of Christians changed by region.
- `7` [Orthodox Christianity in the 21st Century](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2017/11/08/orthodox-christianity-in-the-21st-century/) (Pew Research Center, 2017): the Eastern and Oriental Orthodox churches today, their size and where they are.
- `7` [Spirit and Power](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2006/10/05/spirit-and-power/) (Pew Research Center, 2006): a survey of Pentecostals and charismatics in ten countries.
- `6` [Global Christianity](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2011/12/19/global-christianity-exec/) (Pew Research Center, 2011): the size and spread of the world's Christian population in 2010, by region and by tradition.
