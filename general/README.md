# General guidelines

Guidance for every Christian, whatever their church, and for anyone who wants to understand how Christians are taught to live. Each rule states what the churches broadly agree on, and cites Scripture and the teaching of more than one church family ([ADR-0006](../docs/decisions/0006-write-rules-only-where-the-churches-agree.md)). The [Bible book pages](../bible/README.md) show where each teaching comes from in Scripture, the [areas of life](../specific/README.md) build on these files, and the [church pages](../traditions/README.md) show what each church teaches in its own terms.

> Entries are sorted by score from 1 to 10 ([criteria](../RANKING.md)): 9–10 start here or fundamental, 6–8 recommended, 1–5 further reading or situational.

## Start here

If you only read three sources beyond the Bible, read these:

- `9` [The Nicene Creed](https://www.englishtexts.org/the-nicene-creed) (English Language Liturgical Consultation): the creed of the councils of Nicaea and Constantinople (325 and 381), the statement of faith shared most widely across the churches, in the agreed ecumenical English text. Its notes explain why the words "and the Son" are in brackets: they were added in the West, and each church decides whether to say them.
- `9` [The Apostles' Creed](https://www.englishtexts.org/the-apostles-creed) (English Language Liturgical Consultation): a creed that grew out of the questions asked at baptism, in the agreed ecumenical English text, with notes on its origin.
- `8` [Christian Ethics](https://www.saet.ac.uk/Christianity/ChristianEthics) (Esther D. Reed, Dion Forster, Rudolf von Sinner and others, St Andrews Encyclopaedia of Theology): how Christians reason about how to live, the place of the Bible, and Catholic, Orthodox, Lutheran, Pentecostal and liberation perspectives.

## Topics

### The shared faith

- [What the churches confess together](beliefs.md): the faith of the Nicene and Apostles' Creeds, article by article, with Scripture and each church's teaching, and where the churches differ.

### The heart of the Christian life

- [Love of God and neighbour](love.md): the two great commandments, the Golden Rule, who counts as a neighbour, love of enemies, love among Christians and love shown in deeds.
- [Reading Scripture](scripture.md): reading the Bible regularly, in context and as a whole, with other Christians and in prayer, and putting it into practice.
- [Prayer](prayer.md): praying every day, the Lord's Prayer, praying for others, thanksgiving, lament, praying together, and praying humbly.
- [Worship and the sacraments](worship.md): worship on the Lord's Day, baptism, the Lord's Supper and preparing for it, a day of rest, and worship with your whole life.
- [Repentance and forgiveness](repentance.md): repenting and confessing sin, forgiving others, being reconciled, not taking revenge, and helping those who fall.
- [Growing as a disciple](discipleship.md): following Jesus, the fruit of the Spirit, the disciplines of the Christian life, using your gifts, friends and mentors in the faith, and keeping going when it costs.
- [Belonging to a church](church.md): belonging to a local church, working for unity, sharing in the church's life, respecting and holding leaders to account, settling disagreements, and protecting children and vulnerable adults.
- [Honest and kind speech](speech.md): truthfulness, no gossip or slander, words that build up, controlling the tongue and anger, keeping your word, and confidentiality.

### Life in the world

- [Marriage, family and singleness](family.md): honouring parents, faithfulness in marriage, raising children, self-giving love, singleness as a calling, and never abusing anyone at home.
- [Money, possessions and generosity](money.md): honesty, generosity, guarding against greed, contentment, debts, and stewardship.
- [Work and rest](work.md): working well, fair wages, the dignity of work, rest for workers, work as a calling, and providing for others.
- [Justice and the poor](justice.md): defending the poor and the stranger, equal dignity, honest justice, welcoming migrants, just structures, and care for prisoners.
- [Care for creation](creation.md): caring for the earth, simple living, pollution and the natural world, animals, praise for creation, and fair shares for future generations.
