# Areas of life

One folder per role, stage of life or field of work, such as leading a church, raising children, working, or using technology. Each folder adopts the guidance the churches have published for that area where it exists, such as safeguarding standards, and adds short, sourced rules only where needed. The [general guidelines](../general/README.md) still apply.

No area has been written yet. The plan groups them in three parts. **Church life** covers church leadership and pastoral care, and safeguarding children and vulnerable adults, probably the most important area in practice. **Home and family** covers marriage, parents and children. **Work and public life** covers the workplace and business, online life and technology, artificial intelligence, healthcare and the end of life, education, the arts, politics and public life, and mission abroad.

## Adding an area

Copy [`_template/`](_template/README.md) to a new lower-case folder and follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md#5-adding-an-area-of-life-a-church-tradition-or-a-bible-book-page). List it here under a `###` heading for its group, which also gives it a card on the website's home page.
