# Marriage

Scope: couples preparing for marriage, married couples, and everyone in a church who prepares, marries and supports them. The general rules come first: be faithful to your husband or wife (`FAM-2`), love and honour them with self-giving love (`FAM-4`), and never abuse or neglect anyone in your family (`FAM-6`), together with the marks of love (`LOVE-8`), forgiveness (`FORG-1`) and truthful speech (`SPEECH-1`), all in [Marriage, family and singleness](../../general/family.md). This page adds what the churches teach together about entering marriage and living it day by day. The churches differ on divorce and remarriage, contraception, same-sex couples and the roles of husbands and wives; those questions are set out in the [family page's open questions](../../general/family.md#open-questions), and further questions about marriage are below. No promise of marriage requires anyone to stay in danger: if you or someone else is in danger at home, contact the emergency services first. This is moral and pastoral guidance, not legal advice: the law on marriage, divorce and domestic abuse differs from country to country.

> Entries are sorted by score from 1 to 10 ([criteria](../../RANKING.md)): 9–10 start here or fundamental, 6–8 recommended, 1–5 further reading or situational.

## Adopted references

- `8` [Carefully Prepared: An ecumenical guide for marriage preparation](https://cte.org.uk/app/uploads/2023/01/An_Ecumenical_Guide_for_Marriage_Preparation.pdf) (Churches Together for Families, published by Churches Together in England, 1998, PDF): practical guidance on how the churches can help couples "prepare for a lifetime of marriage, not just a wedding day", written by a group that included Baptist, Methodist, United Reformed and Catholic members and commended by the Archbishop of Canterbury, the Cardinal Archbishop of Westminster and the Moderator of the Free Churches' Council. It leaves aside the questions on which the churches differ, "such as on the whole question of the re-marriage of divorced persons".
- `8` [Amoris laetitia](https://www.vatican.va/content/dam/francesco/pdf/apost_exhortations/documents/papa-francesco_esortazione-ap_20160319_amoris-laetitia_en.pdf) (Pope Francis, 2016, PDF): chapter 4, "Love in marriage", on love day by day; chapter 5, "Love made fruitful"; and chapter 6, "Some pastoral perspectives", on preparing engaged couples, accompanying the first years of marriage and facing crises.
- `8` [The Sacrament of Marriage and its Impediments](https://www.holycouncil.org/marriage) (Holy and Great Council of the Orthodox Church, Crete, 2016): an official document of the Council, which teaches that "marriage is sacred" and that "The freely entered union of man and woman is an indispensable precondition for marriage", and sets out the Orthodox rules on impediments and mixed marriages.
- `8` [Westminster Confession of Faith, 24: Of Marriage and Divorce](https://www.opc.org/wcf.html#Chapter_24) (1646, on the site of the Orthodox Presbyterian Church): the Reformed confession on marriage, which "was ordained for the mutual help of husband and wife", on consent, on marrying "only in the Lord", and on divorce.
- `7` [Catechism of the Catholic Church, 1601–1666](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P50.HTM): the Catholic article on the sacrament of matrimony: marriage in God's plan, its celebration, consent, mixed marriages, the marriage bond, the goods of marriage and the family as the "domestic church".
- `7` [The Marriage Service](https://www.churchofengland.org/liturgy/common-worship/marriage/marriage-service) (Church of England, Common Worship, 2000): the Anglican marriage service in modern language: the preface on what marriage is for, the declarations, the vows, and the promise of the families and friends to "support and uphold them in their marriage now and in the years to come".
- `7` [The Form of Solemnization of Matrimony](https://www.churchofengland.org/liturgy/book-of-common-prayer/form-solemnization-matrimony) (Church of England, Book of Common Prayer): the older Anglican marriage service, with the purposes of marriage and the vows "for better for worse, for richer for poorer, in sickness and in health".
- `7` [Marriage](https://www.oca.org/orthodoxy/the-orthodox-faith/worship/the-sacraments/marriage) (Thomas Hopko, *The Orthodox Faith*, Orthodox Church in America): Orthodox teaching on "the sacrament of Christian marriage in the Church", on the crowning of the couple, and on second marriages.
- `7` [The Service of Betrothal and Crowning](https://www.oca.org/files/PDF/Music/Marriage/marriage-service.pdf) (Orthodox Church in America, PDF): the Orthodox marriage service, in which, "according to Slavonic editions of the marriage service", the priest asks each partner whether they have "a good, free and unconstrained will" to marry the other.
- `7` [Marriage Guidance](https://media.methodist.org.uk/media/documents/conf-22-pc-28-marriage-guidance_JLVfbMa.pdf) (Methodist Church in Britain, Conference 2022, PDF): the Methodist guidance on marriage and relationships, on marriage as "an exclusive relationship, freely entered into with a life-long intention of uniting two people", preparation for marriage, inter-faith marriages and cohabitation.
- `7` [Luther's Large Catechism, The Ten Commandments](https://bookofconcord.org/large-catechism/ten-commandments/) (1529): the sixth commandment (paragraphs 199–221), on the estate of marriage and on husband and wife living "together in love and harmony".
- `7` [Baptist Faith and Message, XVIII: The Family](https://bfm.sbc.net/bfm2000/#xviii) (Southern Baptist Convention, 2000): marriage, and the duties of husbands and wives, in a Baptist confession.

## Key rules

### Entering marriage

- `9` **Marry only by free and full consent, and never force or pressure anyone into marriage.** Before Rebekah leaves to marry Isaac, her family asks her, "Will you go with this man?" The Catechism of the Catholic Church teaches that consent "must be an act of the will of each of the contracting parties, free of coercion or grave external fear", and that "If consent is lacking there is no marriage"; Pope Francis writes that "only those who freely choose and love one another may marry". The Orthodox Church teaches that "The freely entered union of man and woman is an indispensable precondition for marriage", and in its marriage service the priest asks each partner whether they have "a good, free and unconstrained will". The Westminster Confession allows marriage to all "who are able with judgment to give their consent"; the Methodist Church describes marriage as "freely entered into"; and in the Church of England's marriage service each partner answers for themselves, "I will". ([Genesis 24:57–58](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/GEN24.htm#V57), [Catechism of the Catholic Church, 1625–1628](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P53.HTM), [Amoris laetitia, 217](https://www.vatican.va/content/dam/francesco/pdf/apost_exhortations/documents/papa-francesco_esortazione-ap_20160319_amoris-laetitia_en.pdf), [Holy and Great Council, The Sacrament of Marriage and its Impediments, I](https://www.holycouncil.org/marriage), [Orthodox Church in America, The Service of Betrothal and Crowning](https://www.oca.org/files/PDF/Music/Marriage/marriage-service.pdf), [Westminster Confession of Faith, 24.3](https://www.opc.org/wcf.html#Chapter_24), [Methodist Church in Britain, Marriage Guidance, 10](https://media.methodist.org.uk/media/documents/conf-22-pc-28-marriage-guidance_JLVfbMa.pdf), [Church of England, The Marriage Service](https://www.churchofengland.org/liturgy/common-worship/marriage/marriage-service))
- `7` **Prepare for marriage carefully, with the help of your church.** "Through wisdom a house is built; by understanding it is established", and "Where there is no counsel, plans fail". The Catechism of the Catholic Church teaches that "preparation for marriage is of prime importance", and Pope Francis asks that preparation help couples "embark upon marriage as a lifelong calling based on a firm and realistic decision to face all trials and difficult moments together". The Church of England's marriage service says that no one should enter marriage "lightly or selfishly but reverently and responsibly in the sight of almighty God"; the Methodist Church asks its ministers to engage each couple "in a process of preparation", since "the Christian marriage service is not something to be assumed and undertaken without careful thought"; and the churches' ecumenical guide asks how they can help couples "prepare for a lifetime of marriage, not just a wedding day". ([Proverbs 24:3–4](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/PRO24.htm#V3), [Proverbs 15:22](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/PRO15.htm#V22), [Catechism of the Catholic Church, 1632](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P53.HTM), [Amoris laetitia, 211](https://www.vatican.va/content/dam/francesco/pdf/apost_exhortations/documents/papa-francesco_esortazione-ap_20160319_amoris-laetitia_en.pdf), [Church of England, The Marriage Service](https://www.churchofengland.org/liturgy/common-worship/marriage/marriage-service), [Methodist Church in Britain, Marriage Guidance, 19–20](https://media.methodist.org.uk/media/documents/conf-22-pc-28-marriage-guidance_JLVfbMa.pdf), [Carefully Prepared: An ecumenical guide for marriage preparation](https://cte.org.uk/app/uploads/2023/01/An_Ecumenical_Guide_for_Marriage_Preparation.pdf))

### Married life

- `8` **Keep your promises in hard times as in good: stand by each other in sickness and poverty, in crises and in old age.** "Two are better than one… if they fall, the one will lift up his fellow", and the prophet calls the wife of a man's youth "your companion and the wife of your covenant". In the Book of Common Prayer each spouse promises to keep the other "for better for worse, for richer for poorer, in sickness and in health, to love and to cherish, till death us do part"; the Westminster Confession says marriage "was ordained for the mutual help of husband and wife"; the Catechism of the Catholic Church admits that "It can seem difficult, even impossible, to bind oneself for life to another human being", and teaches that "by their own faithfulness they can be witnesses to God's faithful love"; and Pope Francis writes that "Crises need to be faced together". Keeping your promises never means staying where you or your children are in danger (`FAM-6`). ([Ecclesiastes 4:9–12](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/ECC04.htm#V9), [Malachi 2:14–15](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/MAL02.htm#V14), [Book of Common Prayer, The Form of Solemnization of Matrimony](https://www.churchofengland.org/liturgy/book-of-common-prayer/form-solemnization-matrimony), [Westminster Confession of Faith, 24.2](https://www.opc.org/wcf.html#Chapter_24), [Catechism of the Catholic Church, 1648](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P55.HTM), [Amoris laetitia, 234](https://www.vatican.va/content/dam/francesco/pdf/apost_exhortations/documents/papa-francesco_esortazione-ap_20160319_amoris-laetitia_en.pdf))
- `8` **Give yourselves to each other freely and tenderly, and never force or pressure your husband or wife into sexual intimacy.** Paul asks husband and wife to give each other "the affection owed", and "Don't deprive one another, unless it is by consent for a season". Pope Francis repeats the teaching of Paul VI that "a conjugal act imposed on one's spouse without regard to his or her condition, or personal and reasonable wishes in the matter, is no true act of love", and writes that "Every form of sexual submission must be clearly rejected". The Church of England's marriage service speaks of "the delight and tenderness of sexual union"; Luther's Large Catechism asks husband and wife to "cherish the other from the heart and with entire fidelity"; and the Methodist Church calls practices that are "promiscuous, exploitative or demeaning in any way" "unacceptable forms of behaviour". Forcing a spouse is abuse (`FAM-6`). ([1 Corinthians 7:3–5](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/1CO07.htm#V3), [Amoris laetitia, 154 and 156](https://www.vatican.va/content/dam/francesco/pdf/apost_exhortations/documents/papa-francesco_esortazione-ap_20160319_amoris-laetitia_en.pdf), [Church of England, The Marriage Service](https://www.churchofengland.org/liturgy/common-worship/marriage/marriage-service), [Luther's Large Catechism, Sixth Commandment, 219](https://bookofconcord.org/large-catechism/ten-commandments/), [Methodist Church in Britain, Marriage Guidance, 4](https://media.methodist.org.uk/media/documents/conf-22-pc-28-marriage-guidance_JLVfbMa.pdf))
- `7` **Talk honestly, face problems together, forgive each other quickly, and ask for help early.** "Speak truth each one with his neighbour", "Don't let the sun go down on your wrath", and be "bearing with one another, and forgiving each other". Pope Francis advises couples "never to let the day end without making peace in the family", and says that reconciliation needs "the generous cooperation of relatives and friends, and sometimes even outside help and professional assistance". the prayers of the Church of England's marriage service usually include the concerns "Faithfulness, joy, love, forgiveness and healing"; the Methodist Church asks that relationships be built on "honesty, mutual respect, equality"; and the churches' ecumenical guide to marriage preparation starts with "communication, commitment and handling conflict". ([Ephesians 4:25–32](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/EPH04.htm#V25), [Colossians 3:12–14](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/COL03.htm#V12), [Amoris laetitia, 104 and 236](https://www.vatican.va/content/dam/francesco/pdf/apost_exhortations/documents/papa-francesco_esortazione-ap_20160319_amoris-laetitia_en.pdf), [Church of England, The Marriage Service](https://www.churchofengland.org/liturgy/common-worship/marriage/marriage-service), [Methodist Church in Britain, Marriage Guidance, 1](https://media.methodist.org.uk/media/documents/conf-22-pc-28-marriage-guidance_JLVfbMa.pdf), [Carefully Prepared: An ecumenical guide for marriage preparation](https://cte.org.uk/app/uploads/2023/01/An_Ecumenical_Guide_for_Marriage_Preparation.pdf))
- `7` **Pray together, and make your home a place of faith.** Peter calls husband and wife "joint heirs of the grace of life, that your prayers may not be hindered", and Jesus promises that "if two of you will agree on earth concerning anything that they will ask, it will be done for them by my Father". Pope Francis writes that "A few minutes can be found each day to come together before the living God", and the Catechism of the Catholic Church calls the home "the first school of Christian life"; the Orthodox Church teaches that "The union of man and woman in Christ constitutes “a small church” or an icon of the Church"; Luther's Small Catechism shows "How the head of the family should teach his household to pray morning and evening"; and the churches' ecumenical guide counts "prayer and praying together" among the subjects of marriage preparation. ([1 Peter 3:7](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/1PE03.htm#V7), [Matthew 18:19–20](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/MAT18.htm#V19), [Amoris laetitia, 318](https://www.vatican.va/content/dam/francesco/pdf/apost_exhortations/documents/papa-francesco_esortazione-ap_20160319_amoris-laetitia_en.pdf), [Catechism of the Catholic Church, 1657](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P56.HTM), [Holy and Great Council, The Sacrament of Marriage and its Impediments, I](https://www.holycouncil.org/marriage), [Luther's Small Catechism, Daily Prayers](https://bookofconcord.org/small-catechism/daily-prayers/), [Carefully Prepared: An ecumenical guide for marriage preparation](https://cte.org.uk/app/uploads/2023/01/An_Ecumenical_Guide_for_Marriage_Preparation.pdf))

### Supporting married couples

- `7` **Stand by the married couples in your church, from their wedding day through the years that follow.** "Bear one another's burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ." In the Church of England's marriage service the families and friends are asked, "Will you… support and uphold them in their marriage now and in the years to come?", and answer, "We will." The Catechism of the Catholic Church teaches that spouses who stay faithful "often in very difficult conditions, deserve the gratitude and support of the ecclesial community"; Pope Francis writes that "pastoral accompaniment needs to go beyond the actual celebration of the sacrament" and that "experienced couples have an important role to play"; and the Orthodox Church asks its pastors to stand by married people "to fortify their hope shaken by many hardships". See also `CHURCH-3`. ([Galatians 6:2](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/GAL06.htm#V2), [Church of England, The Marriage Service](https://www.churchofengland.org/liturgy/common-worship/marriage/marriage-service), [Catechism of the Catholic Church, 1648](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P55.HTM), [Amoris laetitia, 223](https://www.vatican.va/content/dam/francesco/pdf/apost_exhortations/documents/papa-francesco_esortazione-ap_20160319_amoris-laetitia_en.pdf), [Holy and Great Council, The Sacrament of Marriage and its Impediments, I](https://www.holycouncil.org/marriage))

## How to apply

- `7` [Marriage preparation](https://www.cbcew.org.uk/marriage-preparation/) (Catholic Bishops' Conference of England and Wales): the bishops' guidelines for preparing couples as "A process which is both flexible and gradual", in stages from the first welcome and the marriage preparation "course" to "Life as a married couple, and on-going enrichment and support". Supports `MARR-2` and `MARR-7`.
- `7` [National Domestic Abuse Helpline](https://www.nationaldahelpline.org.uk/) (Refuge, England): "The freephone, 24-hour National Domestic Abuse Helpline 0808 2000 247", for anyone experiencing domestic abuse, or worried about someone who is. Supports `FAM-6`, `MARR-3` and `MARR-4`.
- `7` [National Domestic Violence Hotline](https://www.thehotline.org/) (United States): advocates by phone, "Call 800.799.SAFE", by chat and by text, for anyone affected by domestic violence. Supports `FAM-6`, `MARR-3` and `MARR-4`.
- `7` [Marriage Guidance, 1–2](https://media.methodist.org.uk/media/documents/conf-22-pc-28-marriage-guidance_JLVfbMa.pdf) (Methodist Church in Britain, 2022, PDF): a warning for anyone who supports couples that "the universal Church's historic emphasis on self-sacrifice has often been misunderstood and misused [eg by abusive partners] in a way that is destructive of the wellbeing of the ones abused [often women]". Supports `MARR-3` and `FAM-6`.
- `6` [The Marriage Course](https://www.themarriagecourse.org/) (Alpha International): The Pre-Marriage Course, "Designed for engaged couples or those considering marriage", and The Marriage Course, "Designed for couples who have been married for at least a year", run "by churches, organisations and online in 127 countries worldwide". Supports `MARR-2` and `MARR-5`.
- `6` [Marriage Care](https://www.marriagecare.org.uk/): a Catholic charity that offers "in-person and online relationship counselling and marriage preparation courses", for couples "of faith or none". Supports `MARR-2` and `MARR-5`.
- `6` [Not your ordinary marriage](https://media.methodist.org.uk/media/documents/Not-your-ordinary-marriage-web-20260409.pdf) (Methodist Church in Britain, PDF): a booklet for couples planning a wedding, which explains that "Because the Methodist Church cares about your marriage, not just your wedding day, the minister will probably invite you to do some marriage preparation sessions". Supports `MARR-2`.

## Open questions

Questions on which the churches disagree. These guidelines don't pick a side. Divorce and remarriage, contraception, same-sex couples and the roles of husbands and wives are set out in the [open questions of Marriage, family and singleness](../../general/family.md#open-questions).

- `7` [The Pastoral Care of Mixed Marriages: Neither Yours nor Mine—but Ours](https://www.assemblyofbishops.org/ministries/ecumenical-and-interfaith-dialogues/orthodox-catholic/ocdialogue-2024-marraige) (North American Orthodox-Catholic Theological Consultation, 2023): **whom may a Christian marry?** Scripture says "Don't be unequally yoked with unbelievers", and also that "the unbelieving husband is sanctified in the wife" ([2 Corinthians 6:14](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/2CO06.htm#V14), [1 Corinthians 7:12–14](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/1CO07.htm#V12)). The Catholic Church requires "the express permission of ecclesiastical authority" for a marriage with a baptised Christian of another church, and a dispensation for a marriage with someone who isn't baptised ([Catechism of the Catholic Church, 1633–1637](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P53.HTM)). The Orthodox Church forbids marriage with non-Orthodox Christians "according to canonical akribeia", while each Orthodox church may apply oikonomia, and forbids marriage with non-Christians "categorically" ([Holy and Great Council, The Sacrament of Marriage and its Impediments, II](https://www.holycouncil.org/marriage)). For Orthodox and Catholic couples, this consultation recommends "extensive pastoral preparation and joint decision-making prior to the formation of such marriages". The Westminster Confession teaches "the duty of Christians to marry only in the Lord" ([Westminster Confession of Faith, 24.3](https://www.opc.org/wcf.html#Chapter_24)), while the Methodist Church in Britain, asked for a marriage between a Christian and a member of another religion, normally supplements a civil ceremony "by prayers in the home", though the couple may also be married in church after "appropriate consultation and reflection" ([Methodist Church in Britain, Marriage Guidance, 33](https://media.methodist.org.uk/media/documents/conf-22-pc-28-marriage-guidance_JLVfbMa.pdf)).
- `7` [Apology of the Augsburg Confession, Article XIII: Of the Number and Use of the Sacraments](https://bookofconcord.org/defense/of-the-number-and-use-of-sacraments/) (1531): **is marriage a sacrament?** The Catholic Church teaches that the marriage covenant between the baptised "has been raised by Christ the Lord to the dignity of a sacrament" ([Catechism of the Catholic Church, 1601](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P50.HTM)), and the Orthodox Church speaks of "the sacrament of Christian marriage in the Church" ([Orthodox Church in America, Marriage](https://www.oca.org/orthodoxy/the-orthodox-faith/worship/the-sacraments/marriage)). The Lutheran Apology answers that marriage has "God's command" and promises, but that "if any one should wish to call it a sacrament, he ought still to distinguish it from those preceding ones", the sacraments "which are properly signs of the New Testament"; and Article XXV of the Church of England counts matrimony among "Those five commonly called Sacraments" that "are not to be counted for Sacraments of the Gospel" ([Church of England, Articles of Religion, XXV](https://www.churchofengland.org/liturgy/book-of-common-prayer/articles-religion#XXV)).
- `7` [Catechism of the Catholic Church, 2380–2391](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P87.HTM): **how should the church respond to couples who live together without marrying?** The Catechism of the Catholic Church calls the expression "free union" "fallacious", and rejects "trial marriages". Pope Francis adds that "all these situations require a constructive response seeking to transform them into opportunities that can lead to the full reality of marriage and family in conformity with the Gospel" ([Amoris laetitia, 293–294](https://www.vatican.va/content/dam/francesco/pdf/apost_exhortations/documents/papa-francesco_esortazione-ap_20160319_amoris-laetitia_en.pdf)). The Orthodox Church asks that members who contract a civil marriage "be regarded with pastoral responsibility" ([Holy and Great Council, The Sacrament of Marriage and its Impediments, I](https://www.holycouncil.org/marriage)). The Methodist Church in Britain recognises "that the love of God is present within the love of two human beings who are drawn to each other", whether "through informal cohabitation or a more formal commitment entered into publicly", and encourages "the renewing and deepening (by whatever means) of their commitment" ([Methodist Church in Britain, Marriage Guidance, 5–7](https://media.methodist.org.uk/media/documents/conf-22-pc-28-marriage-guidance_JLVfbMa.pdf)).
