# Education and schools

Scope: teachers, teaching assistants, school leaders and governors, church schools and colleges, Sunday school, youth and catechesis leaders, universities and theological colleges, and every Christian who teaches or studies; and the churches as they take part in education. The general rules come first: defend the poor and the stranger (`JUST-1`), treat every person with equal dignity (`JUST-2`), tell the truth (`SPEECH-1`), never coerce, pressure or manipulate anyone in matters of faith (`MISS-2`), and respect people of other faiths and none (`MISS-4`). Parents hand on the faith at home ([Parents and children](../parents-and-children/README.md): `PAR-1` and `FAM-3`), and everyone who works with children keeps them safe: report every concern to the safeguarding lead and the authorities (`SAFE-1`), recruit safely (`SAFE-4`) and work in the open (`SAFE-5`), as set out in [Safeguarding children and vulnerable adults](../safeguarding/README.md). This page adds what the churches teach together about serving every pupil, faith and freedom in school, teachers and parents, and the search for truth. This is moral guidance, not legal advice: the law on schools, admissions and religious education differs from country to country.

> Entries are sorted by score from 1 to 10 ([criteria](../../RANKING.md)): 9–10 start here or fundamental, 6–8 recommended, 1–5 further reading or situational.

## Adopted references

- `8` [Gravissimum educationis](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_decl_19651028_gravissimum-educationis_en.html) (Second Vatican Council, 1965): the Catholic Church's declaration on Christian education: the right of every person to an education (1), parents as "the primary and principal educators" (3), moral and religious education in all schools (7), Catholic schools and their teachers (8), and care for "those who are poor in the goods of this world" (9).
- `8` [Church of England Vision for Education: Deeply Christian, Serving the Common Good](https://www.nse.org.uk/s/CofE-Vision-for-Education-2016-89ck.pdf) (Church of England, 2016, PDF on the site of the National Society for Education): the Church of England's vision for schools, colleges and universities, built on "life in all its fullness" and four elements, "Wisdom", "Hope", "Community" and "Dignity", for the common good of the whole community.
- `7` [Our Calling in Education](https://cdn.elca.org/cdn/wp-content/uploads/EducationSS.pdf) (Evangelical Lutheran Church in America, social statement, 2007, PDF): the Lutheran statement on education as vocation, on parents and congregations, public schools, Lutheran schools, and colleges and universities.
- `7` [Bases of the Social Concept, XIV: Secular science, culture and education](https://old.mospat.ru/en/documents/social-concepts/xiv/) (Russian Orthodox Church, 2000): an Orthodox account of secular learning, which "Christian tradition has always respected", and of the relation between the Church and the secular school.
- `7` [The Catholic School](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/ccatheduc/documents/rc_con_ccatheduc_doc_19770319_catholic-school_en.html) (Sacred Congregation for Catholic Education, 1977): what a Catholic school is for, its service to the poor, its openness to pupils who aren't Christians, and the freedom with which its educational project is offered.
- `7` [The Identity of the Catholic School for a Culture of Dialogue](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/ccatheduc/documents/rc_con_ccatheduc_doc_20220125_istruzione-identita-scuola-cattolica_en.html) (Congregation for Catholic Education, instruction, 2022): the Catholic identity of schools today, the responsibility of parents, the witness of teachers, and dialogue with other cultures and religions.
- `7` [Catechism of the Catholic Church, 2214–2231](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P7U.HTM): Catholic teaching on the duties of parents and children, including the right of parents "to choose a school for them which corresponds to their own convictions" (2229).

## Key rules

### Every pupil

- `8` `EDU-1` **Give every pupil a good education, with special care for the poor, the disadvantaged, and those with special educational needs or disabilities.** Moses commanded that the law be read to "the men and the women and the little ones, and the foreigners who are within your gates, that they may hear, learn"; Jesus said: "See that you don't despise one of these little ones"; and Paul writes: "encourage the faint-hearted; support the weak; be patient towards all." The Second Vatican Council teaches that "All men of every race, condition and age, since they enjoy the dignity of a human being, have an inalienable right to an education", and asks Catholic schools to care "especially" for "those who are poor in the goods of this world or who are deprived of the assistance and affection of a family". The Church of England's vision gives "special consideration" to "those who are disadvantaged", and holds that "the equal worth of those with and without special educational needs and disabilities" must be "recognized in practice"; the Evangelical Lutheran Church in America asks its schools to welcome "children of all cultural, ethnic, religious, and economic backgrounds, and special needs", and to "provide scholarships for low-income students"; and the United Methodist Church calls on governments to ensure "that all people have access to affordable, high-quality education, regardless of age, gender, ethnicity, economic status or any other divisive marker". This applies `JUST-1` and `JUST-2` to education. ([Deuteronomy 31:12](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/DEU31.htm#V12), [Matthew 18:10](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/MAT18.htm#V10), [1 Thessalonians 5:14](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/1TH05.htm#V14), [Gravissimum educationis, 1 and 9](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_decl_19651028_gravissimum-educationis_en.html), [Church of England Vision for Education](https://www.nse.org.uk/s/CofE-Vision-for-Education-2016-89ck.pdf), [Evangelical Lutheran Church in America, Our Calling in Education](https://cdn.elca.org/cdn/wp-content/uploads/EducationSS.pdf), [United Methodist Church, Social Principles: The Political Community](https://www.umc.org/en/content/social-principles-the-political-community))

### Faith and freedom

- `8` `EDU-2` **Offer the faith freely, and never press it on pupils: respect the conscience of pupils and families of other faiths and none.** Paul commends himself "to every man's conscience in the sight of God", "not walking in craftiness", and Peter asks for an answer given "with humility and fear". The Sacred Congregation for Catholic Education teaches that the Catholic school's educational project "cannot be imposed, but is offered as a possibility, as good news, and as such can be refused", and the Second Vatican Council that the rights of parents "are violated, if their children are forced to attend lessons or instructions which are not in agreement with their religious beliefs". The Russian Orthodox Church supports "optional classes on Christian faith in secular schools, at the request of children or parents"; and the Church of England's vision "respects freedom of religion and belief", while its schools are to teach "deep respect for the integrity of other religious and belief traditions, and for the religious freedom of every individual". This applies `MISS-2` to school. ([2 Corinthians 4:2](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/2CO04.htm#V2), [1 Peter 3:15–16](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/1PE03.htm#V15), [The Catholic School, 59](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/ccatheduc/documents/rc_con_ccatheduc_doc_19770319_catholic-school_en.html), [Dignitatis humanae, 5](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_decl_19651207_dignitatis-humanae_en.html), [Russian Orthodox Church, Bases of the Social Concept, XIV.3](https://old.mospat.ru/en/documents/social-concepts/xiv/), [Church of England Vision for Education](https://www.nse.org.uk/s/CofE-Vision-for-Education-2016-89ck.pdf), [Church of England, Religious Education Statement of Entitlement](https://www.nse.org.uk/s/NSE-RE-Statement-of-Entitlement-final-October-2026.pdf))
- `7` `EDU-3` **Teach about other faiths and beliefs accurately, fairly and with respect.** "You shall not give false testimony against your neighbour", and in Athens Paul first "observed the objects of your worship" before he spoke. The Second Vatican Council teaches that "The Catholic Church rejects nothing that is true and holy in these religions", and the Catholic school offers itself to all, "non-Christians included", "acknowledging, preserving and promoting the spiritual and moral qualities, the social and cultural values, which characterise different civilisations". The Evangelical Lutheran Church in America asks schools to teach "about the beliefs, practices, and history of different religions in fair and informative ways"; and the Church of England expects religious education taught by "Teachers who have secure RE subject knowledge and are able to deliver content accurately whilst having the ability to challenge common misconceptions". This applies `MISS-4` to teaching. ([Exodus 20:16](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/EXO20.htm#V16), [Acts 17:22–23](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/ACT17.htm#V22), [Nostra aetate, 2](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_decl_19651028_nostra-aetate_en.html), [The Catholic School, 85](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/ccatheduc/documents/rc_con_ccatheduc_doc_19770319_catholic-school_en.html), [Evangelical Lutheran Church in America, Our Calling in Education](https://cdn.elca.org/cdn/wp-content/uploads/EducationSS.pdf), [Church of England, Religious Education Statement of Entitlement](https://www.nse.org.uk/s/NSE-RE-Statement-of-Entitlement-final-October-2026.pdf))

### Teachers and parents

- `8` `EDU-4` **Work with parents, who are their children's first educators.** "My son, listen to your father's instruction, and don't forsake your mother's teaching", and the Law tells parents to teach God's words "diligently to your children". The Second Vatican Council teaches that parents "must be recognized as the primary and principal educators", and asks teachers to "work as partners with parents"; the Catechism of the Catholic Church teaches that parents "have the right to choose a school for them which corresponds to their own convictions"; and the Congregation for Catholic Education calls for cooperation "first and foremost between parents and teachers". The Russian Orthodox Church upholds "the right of believing families to the religious education and upbringing of their children"; and the Evangelical Lutheran Church in America "affirms the essential role of parents and congregations in the faith formation of young people". See also `PAR-1`. ([Proverbs 1:8](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/PRO01.htm#V8), [Deuteronomy 6:6–7](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/DEU06.htm#V6), [Gravissimum educationis, 3 and 8](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_decl_19651028_gravissimum-educationis_en.html), [Catechism of the Catholic Church, 2229](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P7U.HTM), [The Identity of the Catholic School for a Culture of Dialogue, 15](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/ccatheduc/documents/rc_con_ccatheduc_doc_20220125_istruzione-identita-scuola-cattolica_en.html), [Russian Orthodox Church, Bases of the Social Concept, XIV.3](https://old.mospat.ru/en/documents/social-concepts/xiv/), [Evangelical Lutheran Church in America, Our Calling in Education](https://cdn.elca.org/cdn/wp-content/uploads/EducationSS.pdf))
- `7` `EDU-5` **Teach as a calling: prepare well, and let your life match what you teach.** James warns: "Let not many of you be teachers, my brothers, knowing that we will receive heavier judgement", and Paul tells Titus: "In all things show yourself an example of good works. In your teaching, show integrity, seriousness, incorruptibility". The Second Vatican Council asks that teachers "be very carefully prepared", and that they "by their life as much as by their instruction bear witness to Christ, the unique Teacher"; and the Evangelical Lutheran Church in America counts teachers among those who "live out their vocation" in education, for "Lutherans understand vocation as a calling from God that encompasses all of life for all the faithful". See also `WORK-5`. ([James 3:1](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/JAS03.htm#V1), [Titus 2:7–8](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/TIT02.htm#V7), [Gravissimum educationis, 8](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_decl_19651028_gravissimum-educationis_en.html), [Evangelical Lutheran Church in America, Our Calling in Education](https://cdn.elca.org/cdn/wp-content/uploads/EducationSS.pdf))

### Truth and learning

- `8` `EDU-6` **Seek and teach the truth honestly, and help pupils to think for themselves.** "Buy the truth, and don't sell it. Get wisdom, discipline, and understanding"; and the boy Jesus sat "in the middle of the teachers, both listening to them and asking them questions". The Second Vatican Council asks teachers to "stimulate their students to act for themselves", and the Sacred Congregation for Catholic Education warns that "Any school which neglects this duty and which offers merely pre-cast conclusions hinders the personal development of its pupils". The Russian Orthodox Church recalls that "Christian tradition has always respected the secular education"; and the Evangelical Lutheran Church in America expects students to show "diligence, discipline, lively interest, honesty, creativity, and respect for their teachers", and universities and colleges to be "places of free inquiry that exhibit intellectual honesty, the disciplined search for knowledge and truth, and civility". Cheating, copying others' work and falsifying results break `SPEECH-1`. ([Proverbs 23:23](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/PRO23.htm#V23), [Luke 2:46–47](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/LUK02.htm#V46), [John 8:32](https://ebible.org/eng-webbe/JHN08.htm#V32), [Gravissimum educationis, 8](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_decl_19651028_gravissimum-educationis_en.html), [The Catholic School, 27](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/ccatheduc/documents/rc_con_ccatheduc_doc_19770319_catholic-school_en.html), [Russian Orthodox Church, Bases of the Social Concept, XIV.3](https://old.mospat.ru/en/documents/social-concepts/xiv/), [Evangelical Lutheran Church in America, Our Calling in Education](https://cdn.elca.org/cdn/wp-content/uploads/EducationSS.pdf))

## How to apply

- `7` [Religious Education Statement of Entitlement for Church of England Schools](https://www.nse.org.uk/s/NSE-RE-Statement-of-Entitlement-final-October-2026.pdf) (National Society for Education, 2026, PDF): what pupils, parents and teachers can expect of religious education in a Church of England school, which is to be "delivered in an objective, critical and pluralistic manner" in "safe, supportive spaces where pupils can openly explore their own religious, spiritual, and/or philosophical ways of living". Supports `EDU-2` and `EDU-3`.
- `7` [Inclusion: Flourishing for All](https://www.nse.org.uk/curriculum-and-inclusion/inclusion-flourishing-for-all) (National Society for Education): anti-bullying guidance for Church of England schools, with parts on race, faith and belief, special educational needs and disability, and socio-economic disadvantage, because "Every child deserves to learn in an environment where they are loved, supported, and respected." Supports `EDU-1` and `JUST-2`.
- `7` [Our Calling in Education](https://cdn.elca.org/cdn/wp-content/uploads/EducationSS.pdf) (Evangelical Lutheran Church in America, 2007, PDF): its "Counsel to Parents" asks parents choosing between schools to consider "the special needs and gifts of their child" and "whether or not they desire a faith-based education for their child". Supports `EDU-4`.
- `6` [The Catholic Certificate in Religious Studies](https://www.catholiceducation.org.uk/ccrs/course) (Catholic Education Service): a course for teachers and others in Catholic education in England and Wales, with core modules on the Old Testament, the New Testament, the Church, Christology, the sacraments and morality. Supports `EDU-5`.

## Open questions

Questions on which the churches disagree. These guidelines don't pick a side. How the creation accounts of Genesis relate to the sciences of the origins of life, which bears on how creation and evolution are taught, is set out in the [open questions of Creation](../../general/creation.md#open-questions). When children should first receive Communion, and how parents of different churches bring up their children, are set out in the [open questions of Parents and children](../parents-and-children/README.md#open-questions).

- `7` [Bases of the Social Concept, XIV.3](https://old.mospat.ru/en/documents/social-concepts/xiv/) (Russian Orthodox Church, 2000): **what place should faith have in schools that serve pupils of many faiths and none: teaching the faith, teaching about religions, or both?** The Russian Orthodox Church holds that "it is desirable that the entire educational system should be built on religious principles and based on Christian values", yet "respects the secular school", and supports "optional classes on Christian faith in secular schools, at the request of children or parents". The Second Vatican Council "esteems highly those civil authorities and societies which" help families "so that the education of their children can be imparted in all schools according to the individual moral and religious principles of the families" ([Gravissimum educationis, 7](https://www.vatican.va/archive/hist_councils/ii_vatican_council/documents/vat-ii_decl_19651028_gravissimum-educationis_en.html)). The Evangelical Lutheran Church in America holds that public schools "should not sponsor religious exercises such as prayer or devotional reading of religious texts nor advocate for or against any particular religion", but should teach "about religion as a vital part of human life and culture" ([Evangelical Lutheran Church in America, Our Calling in Education](https://cdn.elca.org/cdn/wp-content/uploads/EducationSS.pdf)). In Church of England schools, religious education is to be "distinct from collective worship and non-confessional in nature", with Christianity "the main religious worldview studied" ([Church of England, Religious Education Statement of Entitlement](https://www.nse.org.uk/s/NSE-RE-Statement-of-Entitlement-final-October-2026.pdf)).
- `7` [Admissions](https://www.catholiceducation.org.uk/resources/admissions) (Catholic Education Service, England and Wales): **should a church school give priority to children of its own church?** The Catholic Education Service states that "Catholic schools will always prioritise Catholic pupils in their admissions, but applications are welcome from pupils of all faiths and none." The Church of England's vision speaks instead of "church schools serving whole communities" ([Church of England Vision for Education](https://www.nse.org.uk/s/CofE-Vision-for-Education-2016-89ck.pdf)), and the Evangelical Lutheran Church in America asks its schools to welcome "children of all cultural, ethnic, religious, and economic backgrounds" ([Evangelical Lutheran Church in America, Our Calling in Education](https://cdn.elca.org/cdn/wp-content/uploads/EducationSS.pdf)).
